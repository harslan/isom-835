A likely story · not a proven one

Probably not tourists losing their money

  • Many of these look like block bookings by travel agents and groups, made far ahead and later released.
  • The label "non-refundable" may not mean what we imagine. The data cannot tell us for sure.
  • The lesson: before you trust a clue, ask how it got into the data. A model will use it without asking.
