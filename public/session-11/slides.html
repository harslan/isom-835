Rule 1
How we split

Train on the past, test on the future. Never shuffle.

Rule 2
Which columns we may use

Only what we know at forecast time. Yesterday, yes. One hour ago, no.

Rule 3
What we must beat

A naive guess: same hour yesterday. If a model can't beat that, it isn't a model.

What we learned tonight

Forecasting in six lines

  • Split by time. A random split looked 1.9 times better than the truth.
  • Beat the naive baseline first. Same hour yesterday: 63.5. Our model: 39.1.
  • Forecasting is regression on the past. Lag columns + gradient boosting.
  • Every column must be known at forecast time. lag_1 is a leak in disguise.
  • Look where it misses: 8 AM and holidays.
  • A forecast is not a decision. Forecast + a buffer, priced by the cost of running short.
