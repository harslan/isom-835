Split the timeline

"Why can't I shuffle?"

Each bar is one real day of rentals, from Aug 13 to Dec 31, 2012. Blue days are for training, amber days are the test. Our model is the simplest one possible: to guess a test day, copy the two nearest training days of the same kind (workday or not), hour by hour. Try a random split, then a time split, and compare the average miss.

STATION 3

The 24 hour rule

"Which columns am I allowed to use?"

It is midnight. Everything left of the line already happened; everything right of it is tomorrow. The amber hour is the hour we forecast. Click a card to see which hour it points to. Green means you know it at midnight. Red means it is a leak: it needs tomorrow. Then move the target hour and watch some cards change color.
