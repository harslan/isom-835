The Boosting Explorer

A boosted model is a team of trainees. Each trainee is a tiny tree that looks at what the team still gets wrong and fixes a little of it. Here you play with 1,600 real bookings and one clue: how many days ahead the guest booked. 800 bookings train the team. The other 800 are held back as a mock exam.

STATION 1

Be the trainee

The team starts by giving every booking the same score: 38%, the overall cancel rate. That is wrong for almost everyone. You are the next trainee. You may ask one question: "booked more than N days ahead?" Drag the slider to choose N, then get hired. Hire 5 trainees and try to beat the computer's team.
