STATION 3

Shapes k-means can't see

"What if groups aren't round?"

k-means only knows "walk to the nearest host", so it draws round groups of similar size. DBSCAN looks at a crowd from above: wherever people stand densely, that's a group; anyone standing alone is a loner (gray, real name: noise). It has two dials instead of k: reach (how close counts as a neighbor, eps) and crowd size (how many neighbors make a spot dense, min_samples).

Mission: on the moons, make DBSCAN find exactly 2 groups and send the loners to gray. Then try k-means on the same shape.

The flashlight

"Can I see many columns at once?"

The same 400 real customers live in 3 dimensions (R, F, M). Your screen has 2. Hold a mug under a flashlight: from one angle the shadow is a useless circle, from another you see the handle. Turn and tilt the cloud and watch its shadow. The number tells you how much of the cloud's spread the shadow keeps. The arrows show where each original column points in the shadow.
