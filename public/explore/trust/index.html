"Why did it say that?"

Set the promise

"How sure is it?"

The model may now give three answers: "will stay", "will cancel", or "not sure, a person should look". You choose the promise: how often the truth must be inside its answer. Your mission: your staff can check 600 bookings by hand out of these 6,000. What is the strongest promise you can afford?

All four stations use the same data as the Session 9 notebook: 30,000 bookings from two hotels in Portugal (Antonio, de Almeida & Nunes, 2019), a gradient-boosted model, and 6,000 held-back test bookings.
