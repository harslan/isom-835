The arithmetic: profit = value × (churners caught) − cost × (everyone contacted). Contacting a customer with churn probability p pays when p × value > cost, i.e. p > cost ÷ (cost + value). With a $50 offer and a $450 save that is p > 0.10 — nowhere near the 0.5 that predict() silently assumes. AUC tells you how well the model ranks; it never tells you where to cut.
